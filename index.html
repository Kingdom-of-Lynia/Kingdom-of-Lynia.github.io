History

Reimarian Statehood (January 2024 - July 2025)

Lynia was initially founded as a Reimarian district by then Chancellor A.C Blackwood, in honor of his Grandpa who had died a few months prior. Not much was done with it due to the focus on national affairs, and the fact that nobody else really cared about the provinces. On 13 October 2024, in the aftermath of the Reimarian Revolution that led to A.C Blackwood's resignation as Chancellor, Lynia was made its own autonomous kingdom. During this time, the flag was changed. Shortly afterwards, BloodHunter, an outspoken anti-communist, offered to make the Lynian Freikorps to secure Lynia, which was accepted. The Lynian Freikorps was essentially a provincial milita. Two businesses were made during this time, the Sweet Ruby Trading Company and the Goldenleaf Journal. In March 2025, during A.C Blackwood's presidency, Lynia was reannexed in to Reimar, and it became a district again.

Secession from Reimar (29 July 2025)

By July, the construction of Goldenleaf, Nitnelav, and Pavleia was finished. This project had been worked on for over a year. But then, political tensions in Reimar began to rise. Upon the hypothesis that the earth may indeed be flat, in Blackwood's opinion, Raptor had him put in the mental asylum for spreading misinformation. Then came the Freedom of Information Act, which Blackwood thought was very vague and could lead to abuse of human rights, and the weaponization of the justice system. Blackwood tried to get the law amended, but then on 29 July 2025, things reached a breaking point. There was now too many arguments and too much drama over something so minimal, so Blackwood decided to act on his beliefs, and have Lynia secede from Reimar. On 30 July 2025, Blackwood (officially) declared the creation of the Kingdom of Lynia as an independent state, and began working on it, issuing Royal Proclamation 01 and declaring himself King Bruno l of Lynia, and declaring martial law until more citizens come in to Lynia. After a few days, more and more people began to come in to Lynia, and on 5 August 2025, the Constitution was passed. At the same time, people were seeking diplomacy with the Kingdom.

Confederate Era

On 7 August, the Minister-President, Bloodjäger, proposed a temporary Confederacy between the Alpenreich state and the Kingdom of Lynia. On the same day, around 21:25, the announcement was made. On 28 August 2025, the Confederacy dissolved. The next day, Lynia celebrated its first month of independence. On the same day, plans were made for Reconstruction. An agenda to make Lynia's cities more realistic and not in a flat world. The cities, to demonstrate independence, were renamed to Treffen, Valentin, Neubritannien, and Rechtburg.

The Ultimatum

On 23 December 2025, Reimarian officials, with the return of President Bruhboi, formally issued an ultimatum that declared the Kingdom of Lynia an invalid nation and a breakaway state. Lynian officials responded later, and nothing has been heard since.

The Elections

It was now February (Ziv), the second month of 2026, when elections were held for Chancellor again. In a surprising outcome, neither candidate won, so both agreed to be Co-Chancellors.

More Elections

After that, a few more elections happened, and Elektro ended up winning unopposed.

First Year of Independence

On the 29th day of Ethanim 2026, Lynia celebrated it's first year of independence from Reimar. A new flag and anthem is on the way.

Government

The Kingdom of Lynia is a monarchical government ruled by King John I, operating under a formal constitution with a defined legal and judicial framework. Its civil system features a structured probate process via the Court of Lynia—allowing citizens to pass down assets through authenticated wills, routing estates over $50,000 without a will into probate court, and punishing property theft with federal imprisonment—alongside strict regulations to maintain political stability and server order. To safeguard its institutions, the state strictly limits citizens to a single government position, bars diplomats from voting, and aggressively outlaws subversion through explicit prohibitions on treason, plotting government overthrows, election rigging, monopolies, foreign espionage, and server raiding, enforcing severe penalties like banishment for major offenses.

Business Image

Business

legal economy features a diverse set of industries ranging from entertainment and digital finance to manufacturing and consumer services, with a major concentration centered in the city of Treffen. Entertainment and media are well-represented by firms like Golden Productions and Recht Productions, which handle film and video game production. The economy also supports physical production and trade through automotive manufacturing via Lynian Motor Company and commercial goods via Silk Roads Food Store. Specialized commercial services and financial technologies round out the market through unique offerings like the Kingdom of Lynia Bed Testing Corporation and digital currency trading via Dodocoin, while massive conglomerates like Ezernack Corporation operate across multiple cities with a generalized scope spanning virtually all sectors.

Civil Code

Kin Act

  1. You may write a last will and testament to ensure your wishes are carried out after your death (leaving the server, or something like that), leaving what you want to "heirs" (this includes any property, assets, cash, etc) who will recieve and may carry on your legacy, so long as you submit the said Last Will and Testament to the Court of Lynia.
  2. The will will be authenticated by various means, including by testimony of heirs, and, after the will is written, a handover by the Testator including the testator's identifier (kind of like a social security number), name, and other identifying information and other documents to prove that he made a will and that he did infact exist.
  3. If the Testator did not write a will before his time of death, and had assets over $50,000, it may enter court before a Judge, who will deem who shall get what. If an estate has under $50,000, it does not need to enter probate court.
  4. Shall one's estate and objects be taken from the rightful beneficiaries, and reasonable proof can be given that theft occurred, the party, or parties responsible will be promptly sent to federal prison.
  5. This is the line of kinship (who will automatically recieve everything if a man does not have a will): the children of the deceased, the grandchildren of the deceased, the parents of the deceased, the brothers/sisters of the deceased, the aunts, uncles, and cousins of the deceased.
  6. If the Testator is not in his right mind or has found to be under duress, then his will shall be considered invalid.
